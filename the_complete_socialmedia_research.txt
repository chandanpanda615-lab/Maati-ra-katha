  Yes, the gap is real

  Nobody documents Kandhamal. @fromod12 (93 followers) is the only serious
  account there — posts Karanjikana, Belaghar Block, Phulbani. Everything else
  in the space is either homestay advertising or nostalgia reels.

  The deeper finding on writing

  I pulled 398 posts from 12 accounts with full engagement data. The clearest
  single result:

  The highest-comment post in the whole set — 125 comments — was a village stay 
  reel where every comment said the same thing: "Location?" · "Details please" ·
  "Location please".

  People don't comment on beauty. They comment when they want to go and can't
  work out how. That is your unfair advantage: Sarangada is real, shown, and not
  open. The honest answer keeps them asking.

  Measured, across all 398 posts:

  ┌─────────────────────┬──────────┐
  │                     │ comments │
  ├─────────────────────┼──────────┤
  │ Video               │ 2.9      │
  ├─────────────────────┼──────────┤
  │ Carousel            │ 2.1      │
  ├─────────────────────┼──────────┤
  │ Single image        │ 0.3      │
  ├─────────────────────┼──────────┤
  │ Odia script caption │ 5.2      │
  ├─────────────────────┼──────────┤
  │ English caption     │ 1.5      │
  └─────────────────────┴──────────┘

  Best caption length is 11–30 words. A question adds ~45%. Hashtags barely
  matter.

  What won: @fromod12 got 341 likes on 93 followers with a flat sentence —
  "Karanjikana is a small, remote tribal village located in the Belaghar Block 
  of Kandhamal district." No adjectives, real place named.

  What died: "Escape. Unwind. Belong." — 2 likes. "Wishing everyone a happy Rath
  Yatra" — 1 like. Being at the festival works; wishing people well does not.
  Your docs/BRAND.md already bans this register; the data agrees with it.

  The changes, short version

  1. Name Sarangada, Nuagaon Block, Kandhamal in every caption. Never "a village
  in Odisha".
  2. ~20 words, one concrete noun, one question. The notes column in 
  manifest.csv is already this — you wrote those as caption raw material. Use
  them, don't write fresh.
  3. Odia first, English second. Site stays English; captions should not.
  4. Answer "where is this?" publicly, every time, including that it's closed
  until ~2028.
  5. Post albums, not single frames. Single image is the worst format measured.

  Section 6 of the doc lists what the research does not prove — small sample,
  hidden like counts on two accounts, one account inflating its own comments.
  Worth reading before acting on any single number.
