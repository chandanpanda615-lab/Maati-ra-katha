The Weight of Belonging28 July 2026

Why I am building this slowly

Every version of this idea that moves fast ends the same way. Somebody books a week, arrives with a camera, gets shown a performance, and leaves with photographs of a place that does not exist. The village gets paid and gets nothing.

So I am doing the slow parts first. Who hosts. What happens if someone falls ill three hours from a hospital. Which houses are willing, and which are not, and whether a family can say no in year two after saying yes in year one.

None of that is answered on a website. It is answered in the village, and then written down here.

That is why nothing on this site is bookable. When it changes, this is where it will say so.

Written in Sarangada, Kandhamal
