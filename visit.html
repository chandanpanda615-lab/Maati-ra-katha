Not yet. Here is why, and the road for when it is.

The pilot is not open, and no date is promised. This page says what is true so far, what is not decided, and how the road runs.

Where this stands

What is checked, and what is not decided.

Checked

  • Where it isSarangada, Nuagaon Block, Kandhamal, around 20.227°N 84.124°E.
  • How it liesOne market road, roughly east to west. The road in comes from the south, from Baliguda.
  • The lightSunrise over the fields to the east. Sunset into the forest and hills to the west.
  • The groundDeep, productive soil, and no flood hazard at any of six sampled points.
  • The turmericKandhamal Haladi is GI-tagged and grows in the district. Sarangada does not grow it, and this site will not say it does.

Not decided yet

  • Who hostsWhich household, and who is responsible for what.
  • How many guestsNo number, until sleeping, food, transport and care are proven.
  • WhenNo dates. The first guests will be invited, not booked.
  • The walksAny route — into the forest, or to the turmeric fields of other villages — mapped, permitted and paid for first.
  • House rulesPhones, photographs, hours. Set with the hosts, not written here in advance.

The route

Bhubaneswar to Sarangada. The last of it is road.

  1. One

    Fly in

    Bhubaneswar. The nearest airport, and where most journeys begin.

  2. Two

    Drive west

    Roughly 250 km, most of a day. There is no railway into Kandhamal; the nearest railhead is Berhampur, about 150 km south.

  3. Three

    The ghats

    The plain gives way to forest. The last stretch comes up from the south, past Baliguda.

  4. Four

    Arrive

    Sarangada. A kilometre of market road, the upper market one way and the lower market the other.

Before you think about coming

What to know.

The village

A mixed village along a kilometre of market road. Kondh, Brahmin, Kshatriya and Christian families, the same red soil under all of them. Odia in the market and the school, Kui in the older Kondh homes, Hindi among the young.

What to expect

Plain comforts, honestly. You sleep in the village, not beside it. The tea is too sweet. Days begin before you are ready and end facing the trees.

Who it is for

Anyone who wants a place as it is, not as it is performed. Come for a culture staged for the camera and you will leave disappointed. Come to live inside an ordinary week and this is that.

What it is not

A homestay listing, a tour, or a show. Nobody here is on display, and nothing is put on because a guest is watching.
